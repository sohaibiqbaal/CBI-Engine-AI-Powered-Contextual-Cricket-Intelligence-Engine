CBI Formula Reference

STAGE A — Risk Weight
λ(s) = θ × (W+1)/(B+1)
θ₁=2.6 (Inn 1), θ₂=4.2 (Inn 2)
STAGE B — Utility
U(a|s) = Ω·E[r] − λ·P(out)
Ω = 1 + 0.65·(rank_b + rank_t)/2000
STAGE C — Boltzmann Policy
π(a|s) = exp(β·U) / Σexp(β·U)
β = 1.6 (rationality coefficient)

Market Inefficiency — What CBI Finds That ICC Misses

UNDERVALUED ARCHETYPE
Death-over specialists
ICC penalises low average from frequent dismissals. CBI rewards high-SR decisions in death-over states where aggressive intent is contextually optimal — even if the outcome is a wicket.
UNDERVALUED ARCHETYPE
Associate nation attackers
Players from non-Test nations facing elite bowling (high Ω) who make contextually aligned shot choices are systematically undervalued by volume-based ICC ratings but correctly credited by CBI's opponent multiplier.
OVERVALUED ARCHETYPE
Cautious top-order accumulators
High-average batters who play conservatively in powerplay states — when the contextually optimal policy demands boundary intent — are penalised by CBI. This explains the Kohli/Babar finding.
